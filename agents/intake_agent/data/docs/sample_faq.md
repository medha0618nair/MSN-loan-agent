## Section 1: Core Loan Application Requirements
**Question**: What documents are required to apply for a loan?

**Answer**: All loan applicants must submit five core document categories:
1) Government-issued photo ID (passport, driving license, Aadhaar, Voter ID, or PAN card)
2) Proof of address dated within 90 days (utility bills, bank statements, or lease agreement)
3) Proof of income (salary slips or business statements depending on employment type)
4) Recent passport-size photograph
5) Completed loan application form

Context: These documents verify identity, assess creditworthiness, and ensure regulatory compliance (KYC requirements).
Special cases: Self-employed applicants need additional business documentation including GST certificates and business registration.

## Section 3: Proof of Address Documentation
**Question**: What documents are accepted as proof of address for loan applications?

Accepted: Electricity, water, or gas bill; bank statement; postpaid mobile bill; registered lease/rental agreement.
Critical requirements: Shows full legal name and complete current address; issued within last 90 days; legible.
Rejection reasons: Older than 90 days, prepaid mobile bills, name mismatch, illegible copies.
Related queries: address proof for loan, recent utility bill requirement, how old can address proof be, bank statement for address verification

## Section 4: Income Verification Documents by Employment Type
**Question**: What income proof is required for loan applications?

Salaried: Last 3 months salary slips, recent ITR acknowledgement, bank statement with salary credits (3+ months).
Self-employed: Last 6 months business bank statements, current year P&L, business registration, GST certificate, ITRs for last 2 years.
Display requirements: Full name, employer/business name, income figures, official letterhead/stamp where applicable.
Purpose: Determines eligibility and maximum loan amount based on repayment capacity.
Related queries: salary proof for loan, income documents required, self-employed loan documents, business income proof, bank statement for income verification

## Section 5: Technical Document Submission Standards
**Question**: What are the technical requirements for uploading loan documents?

Formats: PDF, JPG, JPEG, PNG. Max size: 10 MB per document. Multi-page: combine into one PDF.
Quality: ≥200 DPI recommended; all corners visible; no shadows/glare; no cropped edges; text sharp and readable.
Common rejections: Blurry images, partial documents, oversize files, unsupported formats (HEIC, TIFF, BMP), glare obscuring text.
Normalization examples: Phone numbers in E.164 (+91XXXXXXXXXX); amounts as digits only (e.g., 450000); dates—address proof within 90 days, expired ID not accepted.
Related queries: document upload requirements, image quality for loan documents, PDF size limit, how to scan documents for loan, clear photo requirements

## Section 7: Complete Loan Application Process Flow
**Question**: What is the step-by-step loan application process?

Stage 1: Applicant enters personal details; system issues Application ID.
Stage 2: Upload documents (ID, address proof, photo).
Stage 3: Identity verification (KYC checks, face match, address validation).
Stage 4: Income assessment (income docs, debt-to-income calculation).
Stage 5: Credit evaluation (bureau score/history, risk scoring, eligibility).
Stage 6: Decision (auto/manual underwriting, approve/reject/flag).
Stage 7: Agreement and disbursal (e-sign, funds to bank account).
Related queries: loan application steps, how long does loan approval take, processing stages, what happens after document upload

## Section 8: Document Verification Timeline and Processing Duration
**Question**: How long does document verification and loan approval take?

Automated KYC: 2–5 minutes (real-time checks).
Document quality review: 24–48 business hours (clarity/completeness).
Income/credit assessment: 24–48 business hours.
Final decision: 48–72 total business hours from complete submission.
Common delays: blurry uploads, name mismatches, missing pages, address proof >90 days, incomplete income docs, holidays/weekends.
Tracking: Use Application ID in portal.
Related queries: how long for loan approval, verification timeline, why is my application taking long, loan processing time

## Section 9: Common Document Issues and Resolution Steps
**Question**: What are common document problems and how to fix them?

Issue: Blurry/illegible → Re-upload 200+ DPI, better lighting, all corners visible.
Issue: Name mismatch → Provide legal name-change/marriage certificate.
Issue: Expired/old address proof → Upload document issued within 90 days.
Issue: Incomplete multi-page docs → Upload all pages in one PDF.
Issue: Unsupported format → Convert to PDF/JPG/PNG.
Issue: File >10 MB → Compress while keeping readability.
Issue: Cropped/partial → Retake with full margins.
Related queries: document rejected, why declined, name mismatch issue, fix verification error, re-upload documents

## Section 10: Best Practices to Prevent Application Delays
**Question**: How can applicants avoid delays?

Preparation: Use scanner or doc-scan app; 200+ DPI; check dates ≤90 days; ensure name consistency.
Upload checklist: All corners visible; no glare/shadows; flat document; color for IDs; under 10 MB; PDF/JPG/PNG.
Info accuracy: Name matches ID; address matches proof; current phone/email; income figures match docs.
Completeness: All pages of statements; both sides of ID if applicable; include cover/schedules for tax returns; add self-employed supporting docs.
Timing: Apply business days; avoid peak month-end; allow extra time during holidays.
Related queries: avoid loan delays, document checklist, tips for faster approval, prevent document rejection

## Section 11: Original Documents vs Copies
**Question**: Are original physical documents required?

Initial submission: High-quality scans/photos or official digital copies are accepted.
Originals may be requested: For final verification, in-person signing (some lenders), unclear copies, higher loan thresholds, or manual underwriting.
When originals are requested: Notified via email/SMS; verified then returned; copies retained.
Important: Keep originals safe; do not mail originals.
Related queries: do I need original documents, are copies acceptable, when are originals needed

## Section 12: Data Security and Privacy Protection
**Question**: How are uploaded documents secured and used?

Usage: KYC, address validation, income/eligibility calculation, fraud/risk checks, regulatory compliance.
Security: 256-bit SSL in transit; encrypted storage; access restricted; MFA; regular audits/pen tests.
Privacy: No third-party sharing except bureaus as required; no marketing use; retention per regulation; auto-deletion after retention.
Applicant rights: Request copy; request deletion post-completion (subject to legal retention); transparency on usage; report security concerns.
