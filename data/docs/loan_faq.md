# Loan Application FAQ

## General Information

### What types of loans do you offer?
We offer personal loans, home loans, business loans, and education loans. Personal loans range from ₹50,000 to ₹10,00,000 based on your income and credit profile.

### What is the eligibility criteria?
**For Personal Loans:**
- Age: 21-60 years
- Minimum monthly income: ₹25,000
- Employment: Salaried or self-employed
- Credit score: 650 or above

### What documents are required?
**Mandatory Documents:**
1. PAN Card (Permanent Account Number)
2. Aadhaar Card
3. Salary slips for last 3 months (for salaried)
4. Bank statements for last 6 months
5. Employment proof or business registration (if self-employed)

**Additional Documents (if applicable):**
- Form 16 for income tax verification
- Property documents (for secured loans)
- Business financial statements (for business loans)

## Interest Rates

### What are the interest rates?
Interest rates vary based on your profile:
- **Personal Loans**: 10.5% - 18% per annum
- **Home Loans**: 8.5% - 11% per annum
- **Business Loans**: 12% - 20% per annum

Rates depend on:
- Credit score
- Income level
- Loan amount
- Repayment tenure
- Existing relationship with the bank

### Are there any processing fees?
Yes, processing fees are:
- Personal Loans: 2% of loan amount (minimum ₹1,000)
- Home Loans: 1% of loan amount (minimum ₹5,000)
- Business Loans: 2.5% of loan amount (minimum ₹2,500)

## Loan Process

### How long does the approval take?
**Timeline:**
- Application submission: 10 minutes
- Initial review: 2-4 hours
- Document verification: 24-48 hours
- Final approval: 3-5 business days
- Disbursal: 1-2 business days after approval

**Fast-track approval** available for:
- Existing customers with good credit history
- Pre-approved loan offers
- Loans below ₹2,00,000

### What is the loan tenure?
- **Personal Loans**: 12 to 60 months
- **Home Loans**: 5 to 30 years
- **Business Loans**: 12 to 84 months
- **Education Loans**: Up to 15 years

### Can I prepay my loan?
**Yes, prepayment is allowed:**
- No prepayment charges after 6 months for personal loans
- Foreclosure charges: 2-5% of outstanding principal (within 6 months)
- Partial prepayment: Minimum ₹10,000 per instance
- Full prepayment: No restrictions after lock-in period

## Repayment

### What are the EMI options?
**EMI (Equated Monthly Installment):**
- Fixed EMI throughout loan tenure
- Step-up EMI (increasing EMIs)
- Step-down EMI (decreasing EMIs)
- Bullet repayment (for business loans)

**EMI Example:**
Loan Amount: ₹5,00,000
Interest Rate: 12% per annum
Tenure: 5 years
EMI: ₹11,122 per month

### What happens if I miss an EMI?
- Grace period: 3 days
- Late payment charge: ₹500 per missed EMI
- Credit score impact: Negative reporting after 30 days
- Penalty interest: 2% additional on overdue amount
- Legal action: After 90 days of default

## Credit Score

### What credit score is required?
**Credit Score Ranges:**
- 750-900: Excellent (best rates)
- 700-749: Good (standard rates)
- 650-699: Fair (higher rates)
- Below 650: May require co-applicant or higher income proof

### How can I check my credit score?
You can check your credit score through:
- CIBIL website
- Our portal (one free check per year)
- Credit monitoring apps

## Special Cases

### Can I apply with a co-applicant?
Yes, co-applicants are allowed:
- Spouse
- Parents
- Siblings
- Co-applicant's income is considered
- Improves loan eligibility
- Lower interest rates possible

### What if I'm self-employed?
**Self-employed applicants need:**
- Business registration proof
- ITR (Income Tax Returns) for last 2 years
- Bank statements for last 12 months
- GST registration (if applicable)
- Audited financial statements
- Business continuity proof (minimum 3 years)

### Do you offer balance transfer?
Yes, balance transfer from other lenders:
- Lower interest rates
- Top-up loan options
- Processing fee waived (special offers)
- No prepayment charges
